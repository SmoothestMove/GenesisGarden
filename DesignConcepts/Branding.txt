BRANDING SHEET — Genesis Garden
1. Brand Identity Overview

Organization Name
Genesis Garden

Tagline / Core Promise
Cultivating Hope, Empowering Dreams

Mission
To eliminate the effects of poverty by providing safe, stable housing solutions, individualized support, and community-centered resources that empower families to thrive.

Vision
A resilient and hopeful community where everyone has access to stable housing and the support needed to flourish.

Core Values
• Empowerment
• Compassion
• Strengths-focused support
• Community partnership and dignity-centered services

Target Audiences
• Individuals and families experiencing housing instability
• Donors and volunteers
• Partner agencies (social services, housing networks)
• Local community members

2. Program Pillars

Housing Stability & Support
• Eviction prevention
• Emergency shelter
• Rapid re-housing
• Permanent supportive housing
• Wrap-around case management

Community & Relationship Strengthening
• Summer Meals for Kids program
• Family Dinner Project
• Open Table volunteer support partnerships

Community Engagement
• Donation drives (furniture, household goods)
• Volunteer pathways (housing support, meals, mentoring)

3. Brand Voice & Messaging

Brand Voice Attributes
• Compassionate
• Empowering and uplifting
• Straightforward and supportive
• Community-centric

Primary Messaging Example
“Everyone deserves a safe, stable place to call home. Genesis Garden walks with families on the path from crisis to stability — offering practical support, compassionate guidance, and community-rooted resources designed for lasting resilience.”

Secondary Messaging Example
“Together, we cultivate not just housing, but belonging — nurturing hope, building connections, and empowering dreams.”

4. Logo Concepts (descriptions to guide design)
A. Primary / Hybrid Logo

Concept
A garden motif blending a stylized house silhouette with leaf/growth elements rising upward like tender shoots — symbolizing growth, shelter, and hope.
Typography
Modern, clean sans-serif for strong readability (e.g., Montserrat or Open Sans) with a warm accent color in the tagline.
Color Palette
• Deep Forest Green (trust, stability) #2A5F3E
• Warm Earth Brown (grounded support) #7A5A3C
• Golden Yellow Accent (hope, optimism) #EDB84E

B. Wordmark (Text-Based)

GenesisGarden
With a subtle leaf element emerging from a letter (like the “G”) to communicate growth and renewal without heavy graphic elements.

C. Logo Symbol / Favicon

Standalone Mark
A simplified icon of a sprouting seed/house silhouette combo.
Should be legible at tiny sizes (favicon, social profile icons).

6. Iconography & Style

Icon Style
• Line-based with meaningful simplicity
• Icons representing home, growth, hands/helping, community circle, meal sharing

Imagery Style
• Real community photos with natural lighting
• Candid images of people engaging (meals, housing support, shared moments)
• Consistent color warmth across photography

7. Favicon & Scalable Assets

Favicon
• Simplified sprout-house icon on solid deep green (#2A5F3E) background

Logo Variants

Primary Full Logo: Graphic + Brand Wordmark + Tagline

Secondary Lockup: Wordmark + small sprout icon (for banners)

Favicon / App Icon: Sprout-house symbol

8. Usage Guidelines

Clear Space Rule
• Keep a minimum buffer zone around the logo equal to the height of the “G” in Genesis Garden.

Incorrect Usage
• Don’t distort or stretch the logo
• Avoid using unrelated colors outside the approved palette
• Don’t place logo on busy backgrounds — ensure legibility
